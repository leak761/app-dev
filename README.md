# Person of Interest

**Person of Interest** is an American *sci-fi crime thriller* series created by Jonathan Nolan. It aired on CBS from 2011 to 2016.

## Synopsis

A genius programmer named Harold Finch builds an AI called **The Machine** that predicts violent crimes before they happen. He teams up with former CIA operative John Reese to stop them.

## Main Characters

- John Reese
- Harold Finch
- Joss Carter
- Lionel Fusco

## Why I Like It

1. Interesting story about AI and surveillance
2. Great characters
3. Mix of action and mystery
